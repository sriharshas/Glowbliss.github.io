Book an Appointment

Got questions?

How do I book an appointment?

Use the "Book Now" button — pick your services, a date and a time. Your request is sent directly to us on WhatsApp and we'll confirm promptly.

Can I modify or cancel my appointment?

Yes. Modification or cancellation is possible up to 24 hours before your scheduled appointment.

Is facial product tested before applying?

Yes, absolutely. Even if the same product was used in a previous session, we recommend and enforce a patch test behind the ear and wait 10 minutes before proceeding.
