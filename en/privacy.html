3. Who receives your data

We only share your data with services we need to run the website and the salon:

  • WhatsApp / Meta: if you choose “Send via WhatsApp”, your request is sent from your own WhatsApp account to ours, under WhatsApp’s own privacy terms.
  • Mobile network providers: if you choose “Send via SMS”, your request is sent as a normal text message through your and our mobile providers.
  • Google: our mailbox (Gmail); Google Analytics, only if you accept cookies; and the Google Maps map on our home page, only if you click “Show map”.
  • Our accountant, for invoices and bookkeeping.

Some of these companies process data outside the European Union, including in the United States. Where this happens, it is done with the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

4. How long we keep it

  • Booking requests and messages: up to 12 months after your last appointment or contact, then deleted.
  • Invoices and payment records: 7 years, because Dutch tax law requires this.
  • Google Analytics data: kept by Google for up to 14 months.

5. Cookies

When you first visit, we ask whether you accept Google Analytics cookies. If you click Reject, or make no choice, Google Analytics is not loaded and does not place cookies.

Your browser also remembers a few settings on your own device, without sending them to us: your cookie choice and the colour theme you picked. These are needed for the website to work as you asked.

The map on our home page is provided by Google Maps. It only loads when you click Show map. Once it loads, Google may place its own cookies. See Google’s privacy policy.

You can change your cookie choice at any time: open cookie settings.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you;
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • have your data deleted, unless we must keep it by law;
  • limit or object to how we use your data;
  • receive your data in a form you can pass on to someone else;
  • withdraw your consent for cookies at any time, without affecting anything done before.

To use these rights, contact us using the details in section 1. We will reply within one month.

If you are not happy with how we handle your data, please tell us first. You also have the right to file a complaint with the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

7. Changes

We may update this statement, for example when we start using a new service. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
