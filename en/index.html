Got questions?

How do I book an appointment?

Use the “Book Now” button — pick your services, a date and a time. Your request opens on your phone as a WhatsApp message or a text message (SMS) to us, ready to send, and we'll confirm promptly.

Can I modify or cancel my appointment?

Yes. Modification or cancellation is possible up to 24 hours before your scheduled appointment.

Is facial product tested before applying?

Yes, absolutely. Even if the same product was used in a previous session, we recommend and enforce a patch test behind the ear and wait 10 minutes before proceeding.

Is Glow Bliss for women only?

Yes, Glow Bliss is a beauty salon for women only.

Where is the salon and when are you open?

You'll find us at H.J.A. Hovens Grevestraat 15, 1333 KX Almere Buiten. We are open every day from 10:00 to 19:30.
